Rust standard library notices
=============================

Toolchain: 1.96.0
Target: aarch64-apple-darwin
Rust source commit: ac68faa20c58cbccd01ee7208bf3b6e93a7d7f96

Scope:
- The 19 sysroot rlibs are matched to a release muu-ffi staticlib before final linking. LTO/dead stripping is not used to omit notices.
- All 144 non-Rust object members in that staticlib are present in compiler_builtins, including the aarch64 LSE assembly variants.
- COPYRIGHT-library.html is the complete, unmodified upstream source report for all platforms; it is provenance, not a list of components in this macOS app.
- Fuchsia, vex-sdk, WASI, Windows, SGX, dlmalloc and test/build-only cc/shlex/rand packages in that report are outside this target scope.
- hashbrown disables default features; its optional foldhash default-hasher is not enabled by std. Unwind uses the macOS system unwinder, not a bundled libunwind.
- The fixed library/Cargo.lock and the actual rlib inventory identify ten registry packages. Five (addr2line/adler2/memchr/miniz_oxide/object) are not separately listed in COPYRIGHT-library.html; their public archive license originals supplement that report.
- compiler_builtins has MIT AND Apache-2.0 WITH LLVM-exception AND (MIT OR Apache-2.0) terms; its complete original, compiler-rt license/credits and bundled libm notice are preserved. It is not classified as MIT-only.
- Compiler-rt and libm full notices include historical/conditional license text; inclusion preserves upstream wording without claiming every described implementation is linked.
