Optional conversion model zenz-v3.2-small (not bundled)
=====================================================

Source: https://huggingface.co/Miwa-Keita/zenz-v3.2-small-gguf
Revision: c67e03e07d215c869f591b274c1631170d3e11fe
File: ggml-model-Q5_K_M.gguf
SHA-256: 29c223d4c23327b80fd13ebb5ab2555057a46317997d5da391584ffbef0db673

The pinned model card declares Apache-2.0 for the model. Muu IME follows
those published terms; responsibility for upstream model lineage rests with
the zenz publisher.

The tokenizer vocabulary and merges embedded in the same GGUF originate from
ku-nlp/gpt2-small-japanese-char, revision
f1623eb5e26cee239d8fc5a661c48482811b3dbb:
https://huggingface.co/ku-nlp/gpt2-small-japanese-char
That tokenizer is licensed under CC BY-SA 4.0, separately from the
Apache-2.0 model weights.

Muu IME does not bundle the model. Users explicitly download the pinned
GGUF from the source above through About; it is not acquired automatically.

GGUF Q5_K / Q6_K unpacking equations and GPT-2 byte-BPE behavior follow the
azooKey llama.cpp fork b4846, commit 10131b23ee0dbd9d03dfc618cec9fe3402919277:
https://github.com/ensan-hcl/llama.cpp
Muu IME does not link llama.cpp; the implemented algorithms retain its notice.
